What is inside

## Three connected databases and one dashboard.

### Clients

Name, platform, status and what they need. One row per client, with contact details kept in the same place.

### Projects

Each job linked to its client, with a status, deadline, rate and a paid checkbox with the payment date.

### Outreach

Every DM, email and call you send, with the response and a follow-up date so you know who to contact next.

### Dashboard

Active work, follow-ups due and delivered-but-unpaid projects, all on one page you open each morning.

How it works

### 1. Duplicate

Open the template link and choose Duplicate to copy it into your own Notion.

### 2. Delete the samples

Remove the sample rows. They show how the links between databases work.

### 3. Add your clients

Add each client once in Clients. Then link projects and outreach to them.

### 4. Use the dashboard

Check Follow-ups due and Delivered but unpaid every day. Those are the two things that cost money when ignored.
